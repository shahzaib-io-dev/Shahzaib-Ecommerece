FREQUENTLY ASKED

Is PCFORGE a real store?
PCFORGE is a fully functional ecommerce platform powered by WooCommerce. You can browse, add to cart, and checkout just like any other store.
How does the compatibility checker work?
Our engine uses real-time data from WooCommerce to cross-reference socket types, power requirements, and physical dimensions to ensure your build is 100% compatible.
Do you offer pre-built systems?
Currently, PCFORGE focuses on custom builds. However, we do offer preset configurations in the Build Lab that you can customize to your liking.
Is there a warranty on components?
Yes, all components come with the manufacturer's warranty. We also provide support to help you with any RMA claims if needed.
